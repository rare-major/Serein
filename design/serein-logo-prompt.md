# Serein launcher logo prompt — Reading Portal

Use case: logo-brand

Asset type: Android adaptive launcher icon concept

Create a completely new logo concept for Serein, a refined Android EPUB reader. Use the Serein reader interface as brand reference only for palette, restraint, editorial typography, and warm paper mood.

Target dimensions: 1024 × 1024 square.

Create a bold abstract “reading portal” made from three nested soft-cornered page frames, slightly offset so their negative space becomes a quiet doorway or focused reading column. Include one tiny apricot vertical marker like a remembered passage. The silhouette should evoke entering a story and layered pages without drawing a conventional book.

Use editorial modernism, a minimal geometric mark, vector-friendly shapes, and sophisticated, timeless, Apple-like simplicity and optical precision. Center one compact emblem with generous negative space. Use thick shapes rather than fine lines and keep all essential artwork inside the central 66% adaptive-icon safe circle so it remains strong at 48 px.

Palette: warm ivory full-bleed background `#FBF8F3`; deep forest ink `#102722` for the dominant outer form; muted sage `#5D7964` for the inner layer; one restrained apricot `#C9744C` marker.

Use flat matte color, crisp edges, and only very subtle paper warmth. Do not include text, a wordmark, an alphabet character, an open-book silhouette, a leaf or botanical form, a ribbon, weather imagery, a generic bookmark icon, a border around the canvas, a device mockup, a shadow, gloss, watermark, or Apple logo.
